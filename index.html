PoJu (Austin) Chao

I am an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. My interests focus on whole-body control, robotic system design, and navigation.

Previously, I received my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from National Central University. At NCU, I led the development of a frisbee-launching mobile robot for the 2024 TEL Robot Combat. The project received the High Distinction Award in the College of Engineering Capstone Project Presentation Competition. I later worked as a Robotics Software Engineer Intern at the Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), where I developed learning-based locomotion and real-time control systems for a panda-inspired robotic platform. I am passionate about building robots, and my career has focused on zero-to-one development: transforming early concepts into functional robotic systems through design, implementation, integration, and real-world testing.
